# WebGPU Renderer — Feature Roadmap

*Origin and path convention: [the crate README](../README.md).*

Continuation of [webgpu-gltf-rust-plan.md](webgpu-gltf-rust-plan.md) (milestones
1–5 shipped 2026-07-18: context + surface lifecycle, glTF meshes, textures +
ACES tonemapping, directional shadows, wasm32 browser demo). This roadmap
covers everything after.

Effort: **S** ≤ half a day · **M** 1–3 days · **L** a week+ · **XL** multi-week.

## Guiding principles

1. **Every feature lands with a golden test.** Headless render + structural
   pixel assertions (the `cube_on_plane.gltf` pattern). If it can't be
   asserted, it ships with a debug view that can be.
2. **Web parity is checked per phase, not at the end.** Chrome's WGSL
   validator is stricter than native naga (see: `textureSampleCompare`
   uniformity) — a wasm32 build + browser smoke belongs in the definition of
   done for anything touching shaders.
3. **The C++ Vulkan engine is the feature mirror.** Where it already solved
   something (CSM, skybox, hot reload, ImGui overlay), port the design, not
   just the idea — and port the *lessons* (resize path, per-image semaphores).

| 🟡 Ray-traced and path-traced modes | XL | The core done 2026-11-15 ([`tracer.md`](tracer.md)): a CPU-built binned-SAH BVH in two levels (repeated and animated meshes instanced, the rest merged in world space), traversed by a Slang compute kernel natively and in the browser, closest- and any-hit queries with the MASK alpha test; the material table and two texture arrays; built lazily off the frame, the 6 M-triangle Colosseum in about 2 s natively. No hardware ray query (owner decision). The modes done the same day: the C++ ray-traced mode and path tracer (NEE, GGX and Lambert lobes, Russian roulette, jitter, a running mean in a storage buffer, its reset rules, the white furnace), the untextured metallic factor honoured, tiled dispatches under a watchdog budget, the editor's switch, chip and PATH TRACING controls; an output whose accumulation does not fit the device rasters and reports raster with the reason (`traced_fallback`). Merged with the raster settings, the deferred path and the clouds the same day: a traced frame replaces either raster path; both kernels write the primary hit's NDC depth (the accumulation's alpha, so no binding is added) into the single-sample depth target, which the clouds march to as the C++ engine binds its traced depth; a ray-traced miss draws the sky pass (cube map, navy or procedural, as raster); the model transform and `set_instances` move the traced instances (a new instance count rebuilds the top level since 2026-11-16); F5 rebuilds both kernels and the resolve; the culling counts read 0/0. **On purpose unlike the C++ engine:** a path-traced bounce that misses gets the renderer's sky gradient times the ambient strength and the environment's intensity, where the C++ path tracer uses a fixed RTIOW gradient at half intensity (its raster has no ambient term to match); the path tracer's camera misses keep that procedural sky, not the cube map, as the C++ one keeps its own. Headless Chrome on SwiftShader draws both modes in the web demo ([`docs/testing.md`](../../../docs/testing.md#the-web-demo-in-a-browser)). 2026-11-16, the path tracer's lights: NEE to one `KHR_lights_punctual` light a segment, picked uniformly and divided by its chance, through the raster's shared falloff, range window and cone (`shaders/common/punctual_light.slang`) and a traced shadow ray; a bounce that misses sees the loaded environment cube, else the gradient; the mean restarts when a light or the environment changes. The ray-traced mode keeps no punctual lights, as the C++ one has none. 2026-11-16, a node's and a vertex's halves bind apart, so no buffer of the full Colosseum passes 90 MiB and lavapipe's 128 MiB binding traces it; animated skins and morphs are re-posed on the CPU and their BVHs refitted, so the traced frame follows them. |
| ✅ Volumetric clouds | L | Done 2026-11-15: the C++ engine's clouds with its bugs fixed (an axis-aligned box that Offset moves, a march that stops at the scene depth, one linear-HDR composite over sky and geometry). The mathematics is `shaders/common/volumetric_clouds.slang`, shared with the C++ engine; the 128³ noise is a write-only `rgba16float` 3D storage texture filled once, the march a full-screen pass over the single-sample scene depth, the forward path's resolved one, the deferred G-buffer pass's or a traced frame's primary hit (`render/forward/cloud_pass.rs`). The Sky tab's CLOUDS section and the outliner's eye drive `ForwardRenderer::clouds`. Since 2026-11-16 the clouds march before bloom, whose bright pass composites them first, so a sun they hide no longer glows around them; and on the forward path the depth resolve also writes each pixel's farthest MSAA sample and its share (`Rg32Float`), so a silhouette pixel marches to both depths and mixes the two by that share instead of showing a one-pixel rim without cloud. Open, in [`BACKLOG.md`](../../../BACKLOG.md): the C++ estimator itself, which has no ambient term (dark clouds against the sky) and undersamples a distant slab at 8 steps (grain), so both renderers change together |
| ✅ Cascaded shadow maps | L | Done 2026-07-18: 3 cascades in a depth array, view-distance selection, per-cascade fitting. 2026-11-15: the C++ controls and fit: 1 to 3 cascades over slices of the view frustum up to a shadow distance (the practical split scheme, view-depth selection, texel-snapped boxes whose near plane reaches the nearest caster with the bias scaled to the C++ size), 512 to 4096 texels reallocated on change, PCF radius 0 to 5, strength, the C++ bias formula and per-cascade caster culling |
| ✅ Bloom | M | Done 2026-07-18: half-res brightpass + 9-tap separable Gaussian, strength slider in the overlay; since 2026-11-16 the bright pass thresholds the clouded scene |
| ✅ SSAO | M | Done 2026-07-18: depth-only reconstruction, half-res + 3x3 blur, tonemap composite, overlay slider |
| ✅ Exposure control | S/M | Manual EV done 2026-07-18 (exp2(EV) before ACES + overlay slider). Histogram auto-exposure done 2026-07-20: histogram compute pass → GPU reduction to an adapted EV → tonemap reads it from a buffer, no per-frame readback on the frame path; off by default (`ForwardRenderer::auto_exposure`), manual EV survives as an override through the same buffer. `src/shaders/histogram.wgsl` stays hand-written (Slang WGSL emitter limitation — see BeschleunigerBallett's [`shader-build-pipeline.md`](https://github.com/Kataglyphis/BeschleunigerBallett/blob/develop/docs/source/shader-build-pipeline.md)), so it is exempt from the generated-shader gates; its constants and workgroup sizes are instead pinned against `render::auto_exposure` by `tests/histogram_constants.rs` (pure CPU, no GPU adapter needed) |
| ✅ Deferred raster path | L | Done 2026-11-15, the C++ engine's Forward/Deferred switch (`ForwardRenderer::raster_path`, the Renderer tab, the Render menu, the viewport chip). A single-sample G-buffer pass (`fs_gbuffer`: normal + metallic, albedo + occlusion, roughness + emissive, 24 of WebGPU's 32 colour bytes per sample; a negative metallic marks unlit) writes the depth target SSAO and both cullers read, then one pass lights it full-screen (`fs_deferred_lighting`, the position rebuilt from depth) with the forward pass's lighting module `shaders/forward/forward_lighting.slang` and draws the sky and the sorted BLEND primitives forward over it. Forward stays 4× MSAA and deferred one sample, measured rather than hidden: on seven lavapipe views at 256×256 every channel difference over 24 levels lies on a silhouette or crease, and away from them the paths differ by at most 0.30 levels on average, the 8-bit albedo target's rounding (`tests/deferred.rs`); on the Colosseum, whose crowd is smaller than a pixel, they differ by 6.4 levels on average (`CHANGELOG.md`). It shades with the Sun's shadow settings, draws the cube skybox and its navy, and the clouds march its depth, each checked against forward by `tests/feature_matrix.rs`. Since 2026-11-16 FXAA after the tonemap smooths its one-sample edges (`render::fxaa`, `shaders/fxaa/fxaa.slang`, the Renderer tab's FXAA row: off, deferred only by default, or every frame): on cube_on_plane its edges' gradient energy goes from 1.42 times forward's to 0.83, and every pixel away from an edge stays within a level (`tests/fxaa.rs`) |
| 🟡 Clustered / Forward+ lighting | XL | Tiled (Forward+) half done 2026-07-28: the CPU bins each punctual light into the 16×16-pixel screen tiles its range covers (`render/tile_grid.rs`, range-aware since 2026-08-01, at most 32 lights listed per tile; since 2026-11-15 a fuller tile falls back to every light, and a light without a range reaches every tile; a light's rect now bounds its sphere's exact perspective silhouette, so an empty tile shades no light), and the forward pass iterates only its tile's lights. Clustered (depth-sliced) binning still open |
